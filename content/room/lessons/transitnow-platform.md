# TransitNow: A Real Business Growing in Real Time

*This teaching is a living lesson. It describes a real business — TransitNow Logistics Services, built by the founder of this Room — and it gets updated as the business grows. Watch it change. That's the point.*

## What TransitNow Is

TransitNow is a courier and dispatch business based in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Drivers apply through the app, get qualified, and get matched to delivery opportunities. Businesses use it to find drivers. Dispatch services run on monthly subscriptions.

It is also the Room's **case study in building**: instead of learning from generic examples, you can watch a real business grow here — the funnels, the pricing decisions, the honest marketing language — and borrow the patterns for your own.

## The App (Live)

The live application runs at **https://funnel-qdx9.onrender.com**. Public pages anyone can visit:

- **/grow** — the front door. People choose their path: drive, hire drivers, or build beyond the driver seat.
- **/grow/apply** — a 13-step application. Applicants can save and continue later.
- **/business** — for businesses that need drivers and courier help.
- **/rsp** — the Route Service Provider path, for people who want to build beyond being a single driver.
- **/dispatch** — dispatch service options.
- **/support** — support tickets, including urgent requests.
- **/drivers/onboard** — public driver onboarding page.

Behind the scenes (admin only) sits the CRM: every application is tracked from NEW through CONTACTED, QUALIFYING, DRIVER READY and beyond, and matched to opportunities labeled **"Potential Match"** — never a promise of work.

## Lesson 1: Price the Service, Not the Dream

TransitNow's dispatch service sells two monthly subscriptions:

- **Basic — $50/month:** onboarding, carrier/business and vehicle info review, basic dispatch setup, ongoing dispatch support.
- **Complete — $100/month:** everything in Basic, plus freight/lane preferences, load-search prep, broker/carrier verification guidance, dispatch workflow, pickup/delivery setup, paperwork guidance, route planning, communication setup.

Notice what is *not* being sold: guaranteed income, guaranteed loads, guaranteed routes. Nowhere in the app will you find a promise of earnings. The honest line used throughout:

> "Plan pricing represents the applicable TransitNow service/plan fee. Driver earnings are not guaranteed and may vary based on routes, loads, availability, expenses, eligibility, and other operating factors."

**The takeaway:** When your business touches people's livelihoods, sell the service honestly. Trust compounds; hype burns out.

## Lesson 2: Every Business Needs a Front Door, a Hallway, and Rooms

TransitNow's app is built like a house:

- **Front door (/grow):** one link that goes in your TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook bios. Everyone enters here.
- **Hallway (/grow/apply):** the 13-step application sorts people by who they are — driver, business, or RSP — so the right information goes to the right place.
- **Rooms:** /business, /rsp, /dispatch each speak to one audience, in their language.
- **Office (admin):** the CRM, contract hub, and operations dashboard run the business behind the scenes.

When you build your own thing, steal this structure: one public link, one intake that sorts, dedicated pages per audience, and an office that keeps it organized.

## Lesson 3: Build Once, Connect Everything

The newest version of TransitNow didn't rebuild anything. It *added* — the Grow funnel connected to the existing driver onboarding, which connected to scanning, routes, support tickets, and the community. Nothing that worked was thrown away.

**The takeaway:** When you upgrade your business systems — a new offer, a new tool — extend what works. Rebuilds are expensive; additions compound.

## September 2026: The Money System Went In

The biggest upgrade yet landed this month — and it earned its own full teaching in this same section, *"Charge First, Deliver Automatically,"* because the principle matters more than the feature:

- **Stripe became the source of truth.** Subscriptions, renewals, failed cards, and cancellations now update every driver automatically. Dispatch work only starts for active paid subscribers. Failed cards trigger an automatic "update your card" email — no more chasing payments by hand. The application stays free; only the service is gated.
- **The tiers became real service levels.** Basic ($50/month) gets 2 route matches every Monday — "Start steady." Complete ($100/month) gets 5 matches on day one, refilled to 5 weekly, matched first — "Grow faster." The system reads the Stripe plan and delivers the right tier.
- **Matching runs itself.** Every Monday, paid drivers get matched to the most recent real opportunities in their city and state by email and text — and if nothing real exists, the system says so honestly instead of faking it.
- **The marketing stayed honest.** The tiers sell the growth difference (2 vs 5, steady vs faster), never income. Not one dollar promised anywhere.
